About My Sports Passport

My Sports Passport is a family of apps built for sports fans. Each app lets you check in at stadiums, arenas, and ballparks, upload photos, follow friends, and keep a permanent record of every venue you've visited.

Whether you're chasing every MLB ballpark, every NHL arena, every AFL ground, or eventually every stadium across multiple sports, My Sports Passport helps you track your journey in one place.

How It Works

Every Passport app follows the same simple process.

1. Live Check In

Check in while you're at the stadium, arena, or ballpark. Select today's game, add photos and notes if you like, and instantly record your visit.

2. Past Check In

Forgot to check in on game day? Add previous visits by selectingthe date, the league, the venue, teams, and game details to complete your Passport.

3. Explore

Browse today's schedule, find nearby venues, explore every league, view stadium information, and discover your next game.

4. Track Your Journey

See your statistics, collect every venue, upload photos, compare progress with friends, and build your personal sports Passport over time.
