How it works

Same simple loop, every sport

Every Passport app runs the same way, so once you know one, you know them all.

1

Find today's games

Open the app and see what's on near you, live or upcoming, with a countdown to first pitch, puck drop, or kickoff.

2

Check in

At the game or after the fact — check in live or log a past visit from your history.

3

Build your map

Every check-in adds a stamp to your personal map of every stadium, arena, and ground you've been to.

4

Follow friends

See where your friends have checked in, compare passports, and keep each other honest about who's been where.
